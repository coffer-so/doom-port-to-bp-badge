# Provenance

Original Doom: id Software. Doomgeneric: ozkl and contributors, based on Doom and Chocolate Doom. File-level notices in vendor/doomgeneric remain unchanged; the pinned revision is in UPSTREAMS.json.

The installable app/ package is a separately written Lua raycasting adaptation in the style of Doom. It does not execute Doomgeneric, parse WADs, or include original Doom game levels, monsters, weapon graphics, textures, sounds or music. Its application source, original map, chest and other project-created pixels are GPL-2.0-or-later. The zombie, pistol and wall textures are downsampled derivatives of ready-made Freedoom artwork under BSD-3-Clause. Their complete notice is retained in app/credits.txt; source hashes, the unmodified upstream license and contributors, previews and the offline rebuild tool are documented in docs/assets.md. The title-screen DOOM wordmark is a separately credited original logo; Coffer supplied its mark. Those logos are not covered by the code license; see app/credits.txt.

The title Doom describes the adaptation's inspiration; Coffer attribution identifies this adaptation, not authorship or ownership of the original Doom game.

No Solana OS firmware or its fonts/logos, photographs, credentials, badge settings or personal session logs are included in the upload package. Solana OS is an external dependency under its own license. Other researched engines are referenced in docs/research.md but are not copied into the application. Freedoom artwork does not imply endorsement by the Freedoom project or its contributors.
